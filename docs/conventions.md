# Source conventions

## Required for every bill or law document

- Official title and identifier.
- Full human-readable names for every supported sponsor, cosponsor, and voter.
- URL for each source used.
- A plain statement for material facts unavailable from the checked sources.

## Preferred order

1. GovInfo and Statutes at Large.
2. OLRC Code and classification resources.
3. Congress.gov.
4. Official chamber, committee, presidential, and historical records.
5. Library of Congress historical collections.
6. Secondary public datasets only for documented gaps.

## Prohibited shortcuts

- Do not infer a vote from party, sponsorship, attendance, or aggregate passage.
- Do not infer sponsorship from a yea vote.
- Do not replace a person's name with an opaque ID.
- Do not silently modernize or repair enacted text.
- Do not represent this repository as an official legal publication.
