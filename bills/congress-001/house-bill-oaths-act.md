# House bill — Oaths Act

`house-bill-oaths-act` is a repository identifier. The checked official sources do not supply a historical bill number, so this file does not manufacture one.

## Status

Enacted as chapter 1 of the First Congress, approved June 1, 1789, at 1 Stat. 23–24.[1][6]

## Backers and bill preparation

- **Primary sponsor:** Unavailable in the checked official sources.
- **Cosponsors:** Unavailable in the checked official sources.

On April 6, 1789, the House ordered that leave be given to bring in a bill regulating the oath or affirmation required by Article VI and assigned five Representatives to prepare and bring it in.[3] Full names are resolved against the First Federal Congress member list.[24]

- Alexander White of Virginia;
- James Madison of Virginia;
- Jonathan Trumbull Jr. of Connecticut;
- Nicholas Gilman of New Hampshire; and
- Lambert Cadwalader of New Jersey.

The journal records the members by surname. The full names above resolve those surnames against the First Congress membership. Committee assignment is recorded as a procedural role and is not treated as proof that any one member was the primary sponsor or that all five were cosponsors.

## Revisions

The checked records establish that a bill existed and that the Senate considered committee amendments, but they do not provide a complete set of official bill-version texts suitable for commit-by-commit reconstruction.

- **April 6, 1789 — House:** Leave granted to bring in the bill; five members ordered to prepare and bring it in.[3]
- **May 4, 1789 — Senate:** Second reading and consideration of amendments reported by committee.[4]
- **May 5, 1789 — Senate:** Third reading and passage with amendments; the amendments were returned to the House for concurrence.[5]
- **May 21, 1789 — House:** The Speaker signed the enrolled bill.[6]
- **June 1, 1789 — President:** George Washington signed the bill into law.[6][7]

The complete enacted text is preserved in [`enacted/congress-001/chapter-001-oaths-act.md`](../../enacted/congress-001/chapter-001-oaths-act.md).
